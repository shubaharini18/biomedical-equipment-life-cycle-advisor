# AI-Based Biomedical Equipment Life-Cycle Advisor

## Project Description

The AI-Based Biomedical Equipment Life-Cycle Advisor is a decision-support prototype designed to support sustainable management of biomedical equipment throughout its lifecycle.

The system uses equipment-related information such as equipment type, age, condition, maintenance information, manufacturer support, usage intensity, and known issues to provide a structured lifecycle recommendation.

The project demonstrates how AI-assisted decision support can be applied to a real-world sustainability problem in biomedical engineering.

## Problem Statement

Biomedical equipment requires proper lifecycle management to remain useful, safe, and sustainable. Poor lifecycle decisions may result in unnecessary replacement, resource wastage, increased costs, and avoidable environmental impact.

### How might we use AI to support sustainable biomedical equipment lifecycle decisions so that healthcare equipment management can become more sustainable?

## AI Solution Overview

The system provides AI-assisted decision support through a structured equipment assessment workflow.

### Workflow

1. User enters biomedical equipment information.
2. The system evaluates the available equipment information.
3. Equipment priority is assessed.
4. The current lifecycle stage is identified.
5. Key factors affecting the decision are considered.
6. A recommended lifecycle action is provided.
7. An AI explanation describes the reasoning behind the recommendation.
8. A sustainability benefit is presented.

The system supports lifecycle decisions such as maintenance review, repair, refurbishment, reuse, and end-of-life review.

AI is used as a decision-support mechanism and does not replace qualified biomedical engineering judgement.

## Responsible AI Considerations

### Fairness

The system should apply its assessment logic consistently across different equipment entries and avoid using irrelevant or discriminatory information.

### Transparency

The system presents key factors considered and provides an AI explanation so that users can understand the basis of the recommendation.

### Ethics

The system is intended for decision support only. Final equipment decisions should be reviewed by qualified biomedical engineering or healthcare professionals.

### Privacy

The system should avoid unnecessary collection of personal or sensitive information. Local user information and assessment history should not be exposed through the public repository.

### Human Oversight

The system does not replace professional inspection, manufacturer instructions, safety procedures, or clinical judgement.
